**Title:** [Free Academic Tool] We built an AI-powered Epigraphy & Concordance Engine for Roman, Greek, and Byzantine Coins (RIC/RPC linked)

Hey r/AncientCoins community! 👋

As longtime ancient history buffs and collectors, we often struggled with two major headaches:
1. Worn circular legends where only 3-4 letters are readable ("...NERV...TR...PARTH...").
2. Converting confusing regnal titles ("COS IIII TR P IX") into exact calendar years.

Over the past year, we built an open numismatic encyclopedia and mobile app featuring:
• **Epigraphic Reconstruction Engine:** Regex & Levenshtein matching against standardized RIC/RPC legends.
• **Regnal Date Calculator:** Automatically cross-references Roman tribunician powers (TR P) and consulates (COS), Seleucid A.S. years, and Ottoman regnal years into exact AD/BC dates.
• **Physical Diagnostic Tool:** Analyzes weight/diameter deviations against official flan standards to flag suspicious cast replicas.
• **IIIF Image Viewer:** Deep zoom into coin surfaces to inspect patina, flow lines, and die breaks.

The catalog is completely non-commercial and strictly educational/academic.
